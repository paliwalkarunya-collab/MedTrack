# MedTrack Project Roadmap

## Milestones

### Milestone 1: Application Shell & Layout (Completed)
- Responsive app shell, collapsible sidebar (260px/72px), 72px sticky header, dark mode toggle, routing, empty pages foundation.

### Milestone 2: Dashboard (Current)
- Welcome Banner, KPI Cards (Inventory Health, Critical Alerts, System Status), Recharts (Inventory Distribution Pie Chart, Stock Movement Line Chart), Recent Activity Panel, Quick Actions Panel.

### Milestone 3: Inventory Management & Batch Tracking
- Categories, All Medicines table, Low Stock filter, Expiring Soon filter, Search & Actions.

### Milestone 4: Transactions & Distribution Logging
- Patient distribution, purchases, donations, receipt generation.

### Milestone 5: Analytics, Reports & Settings
- Analytics charts, PDF/Excel reports export, organization settings.
